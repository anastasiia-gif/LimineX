WHAT CHANGED

1. Time is now a lead time, not our estimating arithmetic.
   Concept and execution days stay in content.js because they compute the
   price, but they are no longer printed anywhere. A new C.price.lead turns
   the total into a calendar band: "about a week", "one to two weeks", up to
   "two to three months". It runs from the first conversation to the finished
   part, and the note under the table says so.
   To move a band, edit C.price.lead in src/content.js — nothing else.

2. Prototyping page: the Prices fold shows project and price only.
   The day split is gone from the card.

3. Tarieven: the twelve engineering fields are a collapsible menu.
   Twelve stacked tables became twelve buttons in a grid, one screen high.

4. Open prices float, they do not stretch.
   On desktop the open panel is positioned over whatever sits below it, so the
   card keeps its height and the section keeps its length. Measured: the
   Tarieven engineering section is 879px closed and 879px open; the domain grid
   on Prototyping is 1714px either way. Only one panel opens at a time.
   Under 861px the panel sits in normal flow — a phone is one column anyway.
