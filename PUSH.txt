THE ONLY PLACE PRICES LIVE
  src/content.js  ->  C.price.rate

      rate:{concept:35, exec:20, hoursPerDay:8, learn:2.5}

  concept  EUR/hour for working it out, calculating, choosing an approach
  exec     EUR/hour for building, testing, delivering
  learn    the "we are still getting fast at this" factor. Every estimate is
           multiplied by it. Lower it toward 1 as the team speeds up and the
           whole site reprices itself, including the days shown per project.

  Change one of those three numbers and all 36 engineering projects, the three
  website packages, the add-ons and the three industrial studies move together.
